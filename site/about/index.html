How I work

Ship it

Projects end up somewhere people can use them: {{liveCount}} are live today on Google Play, itch.io, Hugging Face or the web.

Measure it

Performance budgets, profiling and numbers instead of guesses: frame rates on device, allocation-free loops, scan times on real projects.

Test it

Unit tests, soak tests, level solvers that verify every puzzle, and evaluation sets of hand-labelled samples for AI features.

Respect the user

Offline first and private by default: Android apps without the internet permission, encrypted databases and saves, undo instead of delete.
