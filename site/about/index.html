About

I'm Burak Yıldız, a computer engineer. I graduated from {{school}} in {{graduated}}, and most of what I build is in Unity.

My capstone was a VR safety training simulator for Meta Quest 3, with a fire evacuation, a mine collapse and two other modules. TÜBİTAK funded it through the 2209-A program and I presented it at ALEMSAS 2025 in Baku. It runs at a stable 72 FPS on the headset, which took a lot of LOD work, occlusion culling and baked lighting.

These days I'm finishing Project Doctor, an editor extension that finds unused assets and broken references in a Unity project and moves them to a quarantine instead of deleting them. It's going on the Asset Store. I also make small mobile games; Nomiro Merge Kitchen is on Google Play.

Outside Unity I write Android apps in Kotlin, and I've done some NLP work on Turkish text: an emotion classifier and a paraphrase detector, both on Hugging Face. After living through the February 2023 earthquakes I started a project that listens for voices under rubble and raises an alarm when someone sounds stressed or panicked.
